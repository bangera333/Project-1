About

Illustration that starts with a pencil, not a tablet.

Every piece begins on paper, before anything gets near a screen.

I'm Wren — an illustrator working mostly in gouache and risograph, based between Bristol and a very cluttered studio shed. For the last eight years I've drawn editorial spots, book covers, and the occasional mural for people who want their work to look like it was made by a hand, not a template.

My favourite jobs start with a strange brief and a short deadline. I work fast, I show early sketches, and I don't disappear until you're happy with the line.
